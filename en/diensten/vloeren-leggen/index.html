Laminate and floor installation in Zeeland

A new floor changes a room instantly. We lay laminate and the underlay that goes with it in living rooms, bedrooms and kitchens across Zeeland.

How we approach it

A beautiful floor starts with a good base. We clean and level the floor, lay the underlay and then lay the laminate precisely and tightly. That gives you a floor that connects neatly to walls and doors.

What we do when laying a floor

  • Check and clean the subfloor
  • Lay the underlay
  • Lay laminate in rooms, kitchens and hallways
  • Finish neatly along walls and doors
  • Remove the old floor first if needed

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an underlay with laminate?

Usually yes. The underlay evens out small irregularities and adds comfort and sound insulation. We advise what suits your floor.

Can laminate be laid on a concrete floor?

Yes, laminate is often laid on a flat subfloor such as concrete, with a suitable underlay.

Which floors can you lay?

We lay laminate regularly. Have another floor in mind? Send a message and we will see what is possible.

Where do you carry out laminate flooring?

We work across Zeeland, including Middelburg, Vlissingen, Goes, Terneuzen, Zierikzee and Hulst. Live elsewhere in Zeeland? Feel free to get in touch.
