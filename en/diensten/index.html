Our services in Zeeland

From demolition to finishing: painting, plastering, window frames, floors and more. One team for the whole job, from start to completion.

Facade painting

A well-painted facade is more than a coat of colour: it is the first protective layer against rain, moisture and sun. The surface is prepared carefully, the right paint is chosen for the substrate, and the result is an even, long-lasting finish.

Read more

Demolition

Whether it is a single wall or a complete interior, demolition is carried out in a controlled, safe way with minimal disruption. Debris is sorted and removed properly, leaving the space ready for the next phase.

Read more

Facade insulation

A poorly insulated facade wastes heating costs. Insulation is applied carefully and airtight, matched to the facade type, so you notice lower energy use and a more comfortable indoor climate throughout the year.

Read more

Drywall & partitions

A new partition wall, a suspended ceiling or a completely new layout: drywall is installed straight, level and fully finished, including preparation for plastering or painting.

Read more

Window frames

For replacements or new builds, frames are measured precisely, sealed weatherproof and finished properly, so draughts, leaks and heat loss are a thing of the past.

Read more

Doors

From a new front door to interior and cabinet doors: doors are measured precisely, hung level and adjusted correctly, so they close smoothly and keep working well for years.

Read more

Plastering

A smooth finishing layer is the foundation of any successful renovation. Walls and ceilings are plastered flat and smooth, ready to be painted, wallpapered or tiled.

Read more

Plumbing

From connecting sanitary fixtures to pipework during a renovation: plumbing work is done neatly, watertight and according to the applicable guidelines.

Read more

Restoration & renovation

Restoration is all about craftsmanship and patience: original details are preserved and restored wherever possible, while the space is brought fully up to date functionally.

Read more

Laminate & floors

A new floor changes a room instantly. We clean and level the subfloor, lay the underlay and then lay the laminate precisely and tightly, with a neat finish along walls and doors.

Read more

Carpentry & cladding

From a timber garden shed to facade cladding with black boards: we build the frame, provide a good sub-structure and finish everything neatly.
