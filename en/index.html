Frequently asked questions

What kind of jobs do you do?

We do renovation, painting, plastering, drywall, window frames and doors, facade insulation, laminate and floors, carpentry, demolition and smaller plumbing jobs. See all services for more information.

Which area do you work in?

Across Zeeland, including Middelburg, Vlissingen, Goes, Terneuzen, Zierikzee and Hulst. Live elsewhere nearby? Feel free to get in touch.

Do you work for homeowners and businesses?

Yes. We work for homeowners and businesses, for the living room at home or a commercial building.

How do I request a quote?

Fill in the form on the contact page or send us a message via WhatsApp or email. Briefly tell us what needs to be done; we then discuss the job and prepare a clear quote.

Can I send photos of the job?

Yes, please. You can send photos via WhatsApp; that helps us give a good estimate sooner.
